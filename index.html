About Me

My name is Holt Moriarty and I am a Data Science student at Northeastern University pursuing a combined B.S./M.S. in Data Science. I enjoy using data, programming, and visualization to answer interesting questions and communicate insights clearly. My academic work focuses on statistics, machine learning, and data-driven problem solving. Through my coursework and projects I have gained experience working with Python, SQL, Git, and data visualization tools.

One of my biggest passions is sports analytics, especially soccer. I enjoy exploring how data can explain team performance, player contributions, and game strategy. Soccer provides a rich environment for statistical analysis because the game produces large amounts of event data that can be used to measure performance beyond the final score. Metrics such as expected goals, shot quality, and possession patterns can provide deeper insight into how teams perform over time.

In one of my recent projects, I worked on analyzing the performance of the U.S. Women’s National Team during the 2023 FIFA Women’s World Cup. I collected and analyzed match data using Python and created visualizations to better understand how the team performed during the tournament. This project eventually became an article published on American Soccer Analysis. You can learn more about that project on the next page of this site.
