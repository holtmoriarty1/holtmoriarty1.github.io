Featured Project: USWNT World Cup Analysis

One of my favorite projects was a sports analytics study on the U.S. Women’s National Team during the 2023 Women’s World Cup. For this project, I collected and cleaned match data in Python, built visualizations, and helped write an article published on American Soccer Analysis.

The project focused on what the data showed about the team’s group stage performance. It used soccer analytics to compare performance trends and turn match data into a clear written story.
